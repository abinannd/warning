# Regional Outbreak Warning Report
**Snapshot Window:** 2025-12-12 to 2025-12-18

> **Note for Demo:** This snapshot was specifically chosen for a week known to contain a Confirmed event (Palakkad–Chikungunya) to demonstrate the multi-tiered alerting behavior: Advisory, Watch, and Emergency Warning.

## Status Definitions
- **Emergency Warning:** Confirmed sustained anomaly (≥2 days, peak cases ≥2, gap-corrected baseline). Immediate public health intervention recommended.
- **Watch:** High sensitivity signal (≥2 days, peak cases=1). Elevated risk, requires monitoring.
- **Advisory:** Elevated statistical activity (Z-score > 2.0) but lacks sustained duration. Preliminary heads-up.
- **Normal:** Disease activity within expected historical baseline bounds.
